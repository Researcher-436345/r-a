Test-Time Gradient Guidance of Flow Policies in Reinforcement Learning

Zhiyuan Zhou, Andy Peng, Charles Xu, Qiyang Li, Tobias Springenberg, Kevin Frans, Sergey Levine

UC Berkeley · Physical Intelligence

Abstract

Flow and diffusion policies have become a standard tool for imitation learning, yet incorporating them into RL pipelines for policy improvement has proven more difficult. We introduce Q-Guided Flows (QGF), a method that keeps the reference policy fixed and steers it at test time using the gradient of a learned value function.

…но встроить их в RL-пайплайны для улучшения политики оказалось сложнее.

QGF works by pre-training both a reference flow policy and a value function critic, then using the value gradient to guide the reference policy to generate higher-value actions. Because the policy is never fine-tuned, training remains as stable as behavior cloning while retaining the benefits of value-based improvement.

1  Introduction

Generative policies parameterized by flows or diffusion models excel at capturing multimodal action distributions. However, the very properties that make them expressive — iterative sampling and an implicit likelihood — complicate actor-critic updates, which typically require differentiating through the policy's log-probability.

We ask a simple question: can we obtain the benefits of policy improvement without ever updating the generative policy? Our answer is to move optimization to test time, where the critic's gradient acts as a guidance signal during the sampling process.
